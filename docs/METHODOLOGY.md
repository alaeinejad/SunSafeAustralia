# Methodology

Survey reference: November 2023–February 2024; people aged 15+ within ABS survey scope. Data are published aggregate estimates.

Routine sunscreen means SPF30+ application on most days in the past month (four or more days per week); it is distinct from use during a particular outdoor exposure. Sunburn refers to the past week. Attempted tanning refers to the past 12 months.

Table 5 protection measures concern people who spent more than 15 minutes outdoors during peak UV times in the past week, at their last qualifying exposure. Multiple responses are permitted.

The map uses generalised ASGS 2016 boundaries. Major cities corresponds to class 0; Inner regional to class 1; Outer regional and remote to classes 2 and 3. Very remote (class 4) is retained separately without a value: ABS reports no very remote records in the fully responding sample. Other scope exclusions, including Indigenous Community Strata, are described in the ABS methodology; the map is not a detailed survey-coverage mask.

For percentage measures, source values on a 0–100 scale are divided by 100 for Power BI percentage formatting. A displayed 95% interval is estimate ± published margin of error, bounded at 0 and 100%. Differences in percentage points are descriptive and are not significance tests.

Source-sheet and cell references are retained for traceability. Source workbook reconciliation was not repeated during repository packaging.
